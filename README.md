# Awaaz-AI

India's Voice Operating System for Rights, Welfare & Citizen Services.

## Problem Statement

Millions of citizens face barriers in accessing government schemes, legal rights, emergency support, and civic services due to language, literacy, and digital accessibility challenges.

Awaaz-AI is a multilingual, voice-first platform that enables citizens to access public services through natural speech.

---

## Core Features

### SchemeBot
AI-powered welfare scheme discovery and eligibility assistance.

### RightsLog
Legal rights awareness, complaint drafting, and grievance assistance.

### DocBridge
Automatic generation of applications, RTIs, complaints, and official documents.

### SafePath
Emergency assistance and SOS workflows.
